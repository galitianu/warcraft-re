# MOV-0018: two Footmen colliding

[Issue #24](https://github.com/galitianu/warcraft-re/issues/24). **Priority: P3, obvious
in play** (AGENTS.md). Before this experiment footman-v4 had no unit collision: its
Footmen passed straight through each other. It now plans around other units, and all
four variants match WC3 at P3 level ([results](#results)).

**Reference.** WC3 1.29.2 (9231), x86 32-bit, `wc3-1.29.2-9231-x86`, on the selected
Windows 11 Pro ARM64 VM.

**Question.** When a moving Footman's straight path runs into another Footman, what
does WC3 do? Each unit's route, its timing and where it ends up are open: it might stop,
slide past, step around, re-path, or push the other unit. This is answered from
captures, not assumed.

## Design (fixed before capture)

Four [scenario v1](scenario-v1.md) files in [scenarios/MOV-0018](../scenarios/MOV-0018/).
Two `hfoo` belong to Player(0) with acquisition range 0, on the flat 64×64 oracle map.
Sampling is the normal 20 ms for 600 callbacks (12 s), so both units are idle long
before the end. Every callback samples both units' x, y, facing and order. Orders are
issued at callback 0, after the callback's samples.

| Variant | u0 | u1 | What it shows |
|---|---|---|---|
| a | (0, 0) facing 0 → (1000, 0) | idle at (500, 0), facing 180 | Stationary blocker squarely on the path |
| b | (0, 0) facing 0 → (1000, 0) | (1000, 0) facing 180 → (0, 0) | Head-on, both moving |
| c | (0, 0) facing 0 → (1000, 0) | (500, −500) facing 90 → (500, 500) | Crossing; equal distances, so both reach (500, 0) together |
| d | (0, 0) facing 0 → (1000, 0) | idle at (500, 24), facing 180 | Glancing contact, part of the body width |

The experiment shapes (600 callbacks, 0.02 s, two units, no control) are registered by
hand in `EXPERIMENT_SHAPES` of [trace_contract.py](../tools/trace_contract.py), never
derived from the scenarios. Capture uses the Phase 7 command
([capture_oracle.py](../tools/capture_oracle.py)), which now also accepts a registered v1
experiment's own committed scenario and revision.

Out of scope: terrain and doodad pathing, other unit types, more than two units, group
or formation orders, attacking and acquisition.

## What WC3 does

Plots draw WC3 solid and C++ dashed, both after the model
([a](../results/mov-0018/after/MOV-0018-a.svg), [b](../results/mov-0018/after/MOV-0018-b.svg),
[c](../results/mov-0018/after/MOV-0018-c.svg), [d](../results/mov-0018/after/MOV-0018-d.svg);
numbers in [description.json](../results/mov-0018/after/description.json)). Side is
where the other unit lies at closest approach, relative to the unit's own direction of travel.

| Variant | Unit | Passes other on | Largest sideways deviation | Waits (callbacks) | Arrival callback | Final (x, y) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| a | u0 | right (goes south) | −45.84 at 97 | none | 185 | (988.394, −1.095) |
| b | u0 | right (goes south) | −42.87 at 100 | 86–89 | 196 | (986.944, −6.183) |
| b | u1 | right (goes north) | −15.99 at 184 | 86–101 | 202 | (7.687, 1.285) |
| c | u0 | left (u1 crosses ahead) | 0.04 | 83–105 | 206 | (988.129, −0.003) |
| c | u1 | right | 0 | none | 184 | (500.000, 488.161) |
| d | u0 | right (goes south) | −45.84 at 97 | none | 185 | (988.394, −1.095) |

No unit pushes another: idle units never move, and every order ends with order 0.

- **a and d are bit-identical** for u0 even though u1 sits 24 units higher in d. So
  the obstacle is not a circle at u1's position. It is quantised to a grid.
- **Routes are grid paths.** u0's corners lie on 32-unit cell centres (16 mod 32).
  An idle unit blocks the 3×3 cells around the cell it stands in. A unit switches to
  its next waypoint within about 16 units. It stops within [14.607, 15.90) of the
  target, which [footman-v4's 16](footman-v4-model.md#rule-status) had placed just outside.
- **b, head-on.** Both units stop together at callback 86 (1.72 s), about 54 units
  apart. u0 re-plans south at 1.75 s. u1 waits until callback 101 (2.02 s), until u0
  has moved off its line. u1's first route is partial: it ends at (80, 16), short
  of its target, whose cell u0's start blocks. At its end u1 re-plans and walks to (0, 0).
- **c, crossing.** u1 does not react. u0 stops at callback 83 (1.66 s) with u1
  just ahead and to its right. It resumes at callback 105 (2.11 s), once u1 has moved
  past the path, and keeps its straight line.

## Model

footman-v4 now has unit pathing ([World.cpp](../src/core/World.cpp), parameters in
[World.h](../src/core/World.h) and each v4 trace's `unit_pathing`). It is the
smallest rule set that reproduces all four captures at P3 level:

1. **Planning** (on a move order, and again when needed). Each other unit blocks the 3×3
   cells around its cell. An idle unit within the stop radius of the target is ignored
   (b's u1, fitted to one case). If the straight line keeps `line_clearance` 4 from
   every blocked rectangle, the route is just the target. Otherwise an 8-connected A*
   without corner cutting runs over cell centres. It continues until no remaining node
   can tie the goal. The path is then walked back from the goal, preferring diagonal
   predecessors, each group clockwise from east. Equal-cost routes therefore pass south
   of an obstacle on the x axis. If the goal cell is blocked, the route ends at the
   last free node and is marked partial. The node list is string-pulled to the farthest
   visible node, and the final waypoint is the exact target.
2. **Following.** The steering target is the current waypoint, and the unit advances
   within `waypoint_radius` 16. At the end of a partial route it re-plans and holds for
   that update.
3. **Waiting** for a unit ahead. The unit stops if another unit lies within `block_radius`
   62 of its next 32 units of path and is, or within 32/speed seconds will be, within
   `block_lateral` 40 of the path line. While waiting it steers normally but does not
   move. If the blocker is idle, the unit re-plans at once. If the blocker is moving,
   the unit retests every 5 updates.
4. **Arrival** uses `stop_radius` 15.5 instead of 16, and only when no hold applies.

Every constant was fitted to these four captures. Single observations are noted.

| Parameter | Value | Interval consistent with the captures | Evidence |

## Limits

- Four captures on a flat map with two Footmen. The constants are fits, not values
  read from game data, and the tie order and the idle-at-goal exception rest on one
  observation each.
- Not modelled: terrain/doodad pathing, other collision sizes, more than two units,
  pushing idle units aside, groups/formations, and the real pathing map resolution.
- The wait rule's corridor is not unique. Several (radius, lookahead) pairs fit the
  stop callbacks, so a new capture should choose between them (see below).
- MOV-0001–0010 are single-unit captures. Pathing is inactive there, and footman-v4
  still passes 10/10 with identical exact text under the 15.5 stop radius.

## Next experiment
