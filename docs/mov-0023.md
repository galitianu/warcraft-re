# MOV-0023: held-out three-Footman pathing

Issue #32. **P3 question:** does unchanged footman-v4 predict three units meeting,
including blocker processing and partial routes around two moving units?
Target: WC3 1.29.2 (9231), x86, on the selected Windows 11 Pro ARM64 oracle VM.

## Decision rules and tolerances fixed before capture

Use the existing canonical comparator without alteration: x/y/facing 0.001,
order exact, reported clock 0.0000615 seconds; action targets and record sequence
use its existing rules. Compare complete traces, not only summaries. First
canonical divergence is retained with callback/unit/property and deltas.

For every unit report route relative to **both** other units (closest approach,
side), largest lateral deviation, waits, arrival or non-arrival, final position,
facing and order. Route shape is covered by every sampled x/y, not just route side.
Arrival/non-arrival and final order must agree; descriptive arrival and wait
endpoints use the existing two-callback inspection bound, finals one world unit.
These descriptive bounds do not override any canonical failure.

A canonical PASS establishes prediction agreement only for that layout. A P3/P2
failure is a valid negative experiment, not a completed three-unit gameplay gate:
retain all evidence and name a missing mechanism and next falsifiable experiment.
P3 includes a different route/wait/order outcome visible in normal play; P2 includes
several-unit offsets or a few callbacks' delay on inspection. P1 includes only
sub-unit/sub-degree/sub-frame instrumentation differences: document and pin their
failure counts/bounds without relaxing tolerances or pursuing a fit. None of the
held-out results may be refitted into a passing prediction.

**Decision:** a valid negative evidence increment; the three-unit gameplay gate
remains **not met**. a/b/c demonstrate a missing close-occupied-destination
completion behavior: WC3 ends movement 32–83 units from the common goal while the
model retains a move order. d demonstrates missing initial handling in the layout for which the model plans
two partial routes toward cells occupied by the third unit; the
third unit is held before propulsion, followed by different crossing waits/routes.
These are measured missing behaviors, **not established internal algorithms**.
The raw observations do not identify blocker list order, a recorded blocker,
partial-route exhaustion or a distance threshold as the unique cause.
