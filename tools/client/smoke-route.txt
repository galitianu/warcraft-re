# wc3client route-display smoke script (docs/client-route.md, issue #34).
# Run: wc3client --smoke tools/client/smoke-route.txt --screenshots DIR --record BASE
# Waits count render frames (about 1/60 s each with vsync). @N aims at Footman N's
# body; "ground X Y" aims at a world point, both through the live camera.
wait 30
screenshot 01-start
left @2                  # select the east Footman
wait 5
right ground 450 300     # unobstructed move: a straight planned route
wait 20
screenshot 02-unobstructed
wait 170                 # it arrives: the route clears
screenshot 03-arrived
left @0                  # select the west Footman
wait 5
right ground 200 0       # the straight line crosses the idle Footman 1
wait 12
screenshot 04-detour
wait 20
screenshot 05-detour-later
wait 15
right ground -100 400    # redirect while moving: the old route is replaced
wait 12
screenshot 06-redirected
wait 15
key s                    # stop part-way: the route clears
wait 15
screenshot 07-stopped
right ground -300 500    # move again, then deselect while it is moving
wait 15
screenshot 08-moving-again
left ground 700 -500     # empty ground clears the selection and its route
wait 10
screenshot 09-deselected
wait 120
quit
